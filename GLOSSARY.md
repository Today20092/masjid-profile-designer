# Masjid Profile Designer

A prayer timetable designed to remain readable within a circular profile photo.

## Language

**Adhan time**:
The start time of a prayer, distinct from when the masjid holds its congregation.

**Iqamah time**:
The time the masjid begins its congregational prayer. It may be a fixed clock time or a delay after adhan.

**Jumu‘ah**:
The Friday congregational prayer, with a schedule specified by the masjid.

**Theme**:
A preset visual design for the profile image.

**Circular safe area**:
The region of the image where prayer names and times remain visible after the profile photo is cropped into a circle.
